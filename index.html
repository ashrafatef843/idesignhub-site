Why iDesign Exists

Custom design & manufacturing is still stuck in the past

Designing and manufacturing custom furniture and interiors is still an inefficient, fragmented process — and it's exactly what iDesign Hub was built to fix.

01

Weeks, not minutes

Customers spend weeks or months going back and forth with workshops and design offices through manual drawings before a design is even finalized.

02

What you approve isn't what you get

The final manufactured product often differs from the design the customer originally signed off on — with no shared source of truth in between.

03

Zero real customization

Existing furniture & interior e-commerce platforms offer pre-designed products only — customers can't even change the color, material or dimensions, let alone visualize it in their own space before ordering.

04

No way to compare

There's no centralized platform to compare workshops on pricing, materials, quality and delivery time before deciding who manufactures your project.

The Platform

One AI platform, from inspiration to installation

Our MVP is already live on Android & iOS. Choose or upload a reference image and instantly redesign furniture and interior spaces using AI — customize layouts, furniture, colors, materials and finishes in real time, visualize every change inside your own space, compare different color combinations and styles, and approve a complete design specification before it reaches a furniture workshop or interior design office.

This significantly reduces misunderstandings, shortens the design process, and ensures the final manufactured product closely matches the approved design.

Real-time furniture customization

Approved design specs
